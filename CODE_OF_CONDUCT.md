# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AuronQ welcomes technical discussion, testing, review and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code and evidence;
- provide reproducible technical details where possible;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language;
- avoid knowingly misleading security, performance or decentralization claims;
- disclose conflicts of interest when they are relevant to technical review.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is acceptable. When discussing consensus, cryptography, networking or security, prefer test cases, code references, specifications and reproducible results over personal assertions.

## Security-sensitive information

Do not publish exploitable vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe for contributors.
