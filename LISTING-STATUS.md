# AuronQ (AURQ) — Exchange Outreach Status

**Status as of 12 October 2026: Project-initiated listing outreach is on hold.**

The AuronQ project contact has requested that exchanges previously approached suspend review of project-initiated listing inquiries/applications and any negotiations or integration activity proposed **on behalf of the project**, pending an independent legal and regulatory assessment.

- No new project-initiated exchange listing applications or commercial listing negotiations are being pursued while this hold remains in effect.
- No exchange, intermediary or representative is authorized by this notice to commit the project to listing fees, payments, AURQ transfers or allocations, liquidity provision, or market-making arrangements. No such future commitment should be inferred from earlier preliminary correspondence.
- Requests to pause were sent to prior contacts. Their receipt or acceptance **has not yet been independently confirmed**, so this notice is not a claim that every external process is closed.
- Technical documentation remains available. Independent third parties may choose to build software or assess the network without prior project approval, but they are responsible for their own due diligence, operational security and applicable legal/regulatory requirements. This hold neither prevents nor authorizes third-party activities as a matter of law.
- Running Mainnet nodes, mining, reviewing code, reporting defects and developing compatible open-source tools are unaffected.

AuronQ remains experimental and unaudited. This is a **project outreach policy**, not a legal determination about whether AURQ has an identifiable issuer, whether any exemption applies under MiCA, or whether a particular listing would be lawful. The disclosed genesis allocation remains **210,000 AURQ (1% of the 21 million AURQ nominal supply)**.

This announcement does not change Mainnet genesis, Network ID, consensus rules, monetary policy, circulating ledger balances, software release artifacts, or ownership of any AURQ. It is not an offer to sell crypto-assets and does not promise any exchange listing, price or investment return.

Any resumption of project-initiated listing activity will be considered only after a suitable legal/regulatory review and a separately communicated decision.
